HARD RULES
  Never upscale more than 2x.  Measured: 678px holds to ~1356px, dies at 3x.
  Never sharpen after upscaling — it measurably worsened every test image.
  No AI upscaling on masonry surfaces — invented stone texture reads as fake
    to the exact person evaluating a mason.
  No lightbox on the 678px set. Tier A and the Yelp set only.
