About

Building in the Conejo Valley since 1991

35 years of foundations, stamped concrete, natural stone, block walls and brick — from large-scale renovations to smaller residential jobs.

CPF Custom Concrete and Masonry is an independent consulting and contracting company specializing in foundations, stamped concrete, natural stone, block walls and brick products. Clients enjoy the peace of mind that comes with a guaranteed completion date and full insurance coverage. Project expertise ranges from large-scale renovations to smaller residential jobs, with tenacious attention to detail and pride of accomplishment on every one.

In the Conejo Valley since 1991

CPF has been building here for 35 years. That covers several housing booms, a couple of recessions, and a great many contractors who came and went in between — which is most of what a homeowner is really asking when they ask how long you have been doing this.

It also means the work is checkable. The retaining walls in Dos Vientos, the ballfields at Triunfo YMCA and Viewpoint School, the driveways and outdoor kitchens across Newbury Park and Thousand Oaks — they are all still standing, and most of them are a short drive away.

This website is new. The business is not. If you want to confirm that before you call, California contractor license #878989 is a matter of public record and takes about thirty seconds to check on the CSLB license lookup.

What we actually do

Most of the work is residential hardscape across Newbury Park, Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Calabasas and Camarillo — driveways, patios, retaining walls, outdoor kitchens and fireplaces. Alongside that, CPF has delivered two community and institutional ballfield projects: the Triunfo YMCA baseball fields, and Robertson Family Field at Viewpoint School in Calabasas.

Those two matter more than their size suggests. Institutional clients award work on scheduling and insurance as much as on craft, and they do not hand a live school campus to a contractor who might overrun. It is the same promise a homeowner is buying: the job gets finished, on the date agreed.

Two things worth knowing before you call

  • Guaranteed completion date. Agreed at quoting, not hoped for afterwards. A contractor who turns up when he said he would is the thing most people are actually worried about.
  • Full insurance coverage. On every job, residential and commercial.
