Custom Concrete Driveways

A driveway is the largest single piece of concrete on most properties and the first thing anyone sees. CPF pours stamped, colored and paver driveways across Thousand Oaks, Newbury Park and the wider Ventura County area.

The driveway is the first thing anyone sees

It is also the piece of concrete that takes the most abuse: vehicle loads, a full temperature swing every day, and whatever the ground underneath decides to do over twenty years. A driveway that cracks in its third summer was almost never a finishing problem. It was the base, the thickness, or the control joints.

Stamped and colored concrete

Stamping takes a poured slab and gives it the pattern and texture of stone, brick or tile at a fraction of the cost of laying the real material over that area. The result is only as good as the timing — stamps go in during a window measured in minutes, and a crew that misses it leaves the evidence in the surface permanently.

Paver driveways

Pavers buy you something concrete cannot: individual units that can be lifted and re-laid. If a utility has to come up, or a section settles, the repair is invisible. The tradeoff is the base preparation, which is more exacting than for a slab, not less.

Everything the driveway touches

Most of these jobs are not only a driveway. The grade has to be resolved where it meets the street, the garage slab and the front walk, and on a sloping lot that usually means a retaining wall in the same pour sequence. Doing it as one job is why the banding, the borders and the wall all line up — see the stonework and grading and excavation pages for the parts of that work that happen before any concrete arrives.

Owner supervised, fully insured

A concrete pour takes the driveway out of use, and an open form is not something anyone wants sitting for a fortnight. Each project is owner supervised — we are the first in and the last out — and CPF is licensed, insured and bonded under C-29 masonry and C-8 concrete.

  • Stamped concrete driveway, colored and sealed, running the full width of the frontage.

  • Stamped driveway tied into a stone-faced retaining wall on the same job.

  • Paver driveway framed by custom concrete pillars at the entry.

  • Sidewalk banding and borders poured as part of the driveway, not added afterwards.

  • Paver driveway with an integrated custom concrete water feature.

  • Driveway held by stone retaining walls where the grade falls away.
