Featured project · Residential

Dos Vientos Villa

A prime example of CPF Masonry's capabilities for an exterior residential remodel. This job primarily focused on three areas: the front yard, the patio, and the outdoor kitchen.

Dos Vientos Villa is the residential counterpart to the two ballfield projects: one house, one client, and every trade CPF offers turned on the same address at the same time. The grading, the stamped concrete, the stonework and the outdoor kitchen were one job rather than four contractors, which is the reason the driveway, the sidewalk accents and the wall line all resolve against each other.

The Front Yard

The initial design is centered around the unique look of the stamped concrete driveway. The villa is then framed by the custom stone retaining walls punctuated by smooth boulders. The stone pillars draw the eye up to the custom stone, full size walls surrounding the gate. Stamped concrete and stone accents pour out into the sidewalk to truly utilize the available space.

The Patio

The client wanted to combine their love for entertaining with the outdoors. The patio design allows the client to host in an intimate setting in any climate. Custom stone walkways set the tone for the design. The patio is enclosed by custom stone pillars and glass wall enclosure, supporting the retractable awning. This set up affords the option to experience the lovely California sun while blocking any potential wind.

The Outdoor Kitchen

Any host's dream. The custom BBQ kitchen features tiled counter tops and trim details. The custom details include the integrated ice chest and drink cooler, built in refrigerated storage, sinks, propane barbecue and stove top — all built upon a CPF Masonry designed stone patio.

This CPF Masonry remodel gave the home its own identity in the neighborhood. The attention to detail in the design took a solid house and made it an extraordinary home.
