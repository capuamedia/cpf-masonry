Grading and Excavation

CPF comes fully equipped with a complete line of grading and excavation equipment. When we say we will drive your project from concept to completion, we mean it.

Concept to completion, with our own equipment

Our complete fleet of grading and excavation equipment gives us the tools needed to perfectly prepare your property for its upcoming project. Your project is managed by the best in the business, from start to finish.

Why it matters that it is the same company

Preparation is everything in the world of property renovation. Many jobs require the deployment of various different contractors to prepare your property for the project to be done. At CPF, we go the extra mile to cut out that extra step by providing our customers with a complete full-circle service.

Our grading and excavation services make use of our complete fleet of tractors, excavators and dump trucks. It is us who will carry out the project planning process with you, and us who know what the ultimate vision of your project is. It only makes sense that we handle any excavation and grading that may be needed to prepare for laying foundation, reshaping the lay of your land, or anything else.

The part nobody photographs

Excavation is the work that decides whether everything after it holds. A retaining wall fails at its footing and its drainage far more often than at its face, and a driveway cracks because of what is under it rather than what was done to the surface. Both of those are settled before any concrete or block arrives on site.

It is also the stage where the awkward realities surface — access for the machine, where the spoil goes, and what is actually buried in the yard. A contractor who is doing the whole job has every reason to find those on day one rather than hand them to whoever comes next.
