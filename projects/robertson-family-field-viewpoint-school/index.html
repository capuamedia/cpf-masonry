Featured project · Institutional

Robertson Family Field, Viewpoint School

Following the success of our Triunfo YMCA playfields project, we put the wraps on a baseball field build at Viewpoint School in Calabasas, California.

Viewpoint School came after Triunfo. A private school campus in Calabasas awarded CPF the Robertson Family Field build on the strength of the YMCA playfields — which is the useful thing about institutional work: the second one is won by the first one.

A school field carries a constraint a community field does not. The work has to fit the academic calendar, on a live campus, with the site secure and the dates immovable. That is a scheduling problem as much as a construction one, and it is the reason a guaranteed completion date and full insurance coverage are what get a contractor onto a campus in the first place.

Two institutional references

Between Triunfo YMCA and Viewpoint School, CPF holds two community and institutional references — the kind most residential masonry contractors cannot show a homeowner. If the crew can be trusted on a school campus against a fixed handover date, a driveway or a retaining wall is a straightforward proposition.
